Stock Data Analysis Tool

Helping investors gauge market sentiment without manually reading financial news.

Overview

Reading financial news to gauge market mood is slow and inconsistent. This tool automates that read: a transformer pipeline processes financial text, summarizes it, and scores sentiment — surfacing the signal through a simple analysis dashboard instead of a wall of articles.

Alongside sentiment scoring, the dashboard gives users a visual read on how a particular stock is performing, along with a future trend prediction, so the sentiment signal sits next to the price action it's meant to explain.

Highlights
  • Transformer pipeline (DistilBERT, BART, RoBERTa, GPT-2) for summarization and sentiment scoring
  • Simple analysis dashboard surfacing sentiment at a glance
  • Visual representation of stock performance
  • Future trend prediction alongside sentiment signal
