Role Purpose

Why this role exists.

To bridge the gap between academia and industry by enabling students and faculty to effectively adopt Artificial Intelligence technologies for research, learning, creativity, project development, career readiness, and innovation. The specialist creates awareness, provides hands-on training, mentors projects, and establishes AI-driven learning practices across all academic streams.
